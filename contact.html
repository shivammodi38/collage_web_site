Frequently Asked Questions

How can I register for an event?

Visit the registration page, fill in your details and select your preferred events.

Can I participate in multiple events?

Yes, you can participate in multiple events as long as their timings do not overlap.

Where can I check the registration fees?

All registration fees are available on the Events page.

Do I need to carry my college ID?

Yes, participants must carry a valid college identity card.

Do I need to bring my own equipment?

No, all necessary equipment will be provided by the organizing committee.

When will TechQuest 2027 take place?

TechQuest 2027 will take place from 12 to 14 February 2027.

Where will TechQuest 2027 be held?

The festival will be held at Quad AI School of Technology and Management, 3rd Floor, Gigaspace, Patna, Bihar - 800024.
